# GUARDRAILS
The failure modes that would discredit this product. Each is evidenced by live measurement (2026-08-17) and each maps to a blocking test in `CLAUDE_TO_CODEX.md` Part 3.

Claude reviews every chart and every piece of UI copy against this list before release (master prompt §32, §45).

| ID | Guardrail | Evidence | Test |
|---|---|---|---|
| **G-1** | **GDP is not income.** Per-capita living-standards views must show GNI per capita alongside GDP per capita, flagged inline, wherever the GDP–GNI wedge exceeds 15%. | Ireland 2024: GDP **$609.2bn** vs GNI **$457.7bn** (+33.1%); GDP/cap **$112,895** vs GNI/cap **$80,650**. Same mechanism in LUX, SGP, CHE. | `TEST-M-010` |
| **G-2** | **Central government ≠ general government.** Debt is general government, WEO-sourced, one series for its whole history. Never spliced, never mixed across countries. | WB `GC.DOD.TOTL.GD.ZS` = **15/50** and central-only. IMF exposes ≥3 distinct "govt debt %GDP" series from different datasets. | `TEST-M-007` |
| **G-3** | **Selection bias hard-coded into the source.** A metric whose source population is a strict subset of our universe is **structurally barred** from cross-universe ranking — not warned, barred. | `DT.DOD.DECT.CD` = **19/50**; the missing 31 are advanced economies excluded *by design* (IDS covers LMIC borrowers). A ranking on it returns an all-EM list and implies the US has no external debt. | `D-007` in chart layer |
| **G-4** | **The projection boundary is rendered, always.** `observation_class` NOT NULL on every row. No projection enters a CAGR, rank, correlation, or model input without an explicit opt-in and a forecast-derived tag. **Highest severity in the project.** | IMF DataMapper returns `NGDP_RPCH` for **1980–2031** undifferentiated. Verified: `USA 2026=2.3, 2029=1.9, 2031=1.8` — all forecasts, unflagged. | `TEST-O-001`, `TEST-O-002` |
| **G-5** | **Aggregate contamination.** Filter by reference list only, never name matching. | WB `/country` = **295 entities, 78 aggregates**. IMF mixes `WEOWORLD`, `ADVEC`, `EURO`, `EU`, `OEMDC`, `SSA` into the same `values` dict as economies. | `TEST-U-001` |
| **G-6** | **Inequality data does not support the proposed uses.** Gini is Class C: country page only, survey year printed on the value, never on a ranking axis, **never in a composite**. | Clears 40/50 in **2018 only**. India, Saudi Arabia, Singapore, Bangladesh, Hong Kong, South Africa, Egypt, Chile absent even then. Surveys mix consumption/income, gross/net, reference years differ by up to a decade. | `D-010` |
| **G-7** | **Hyperinflation breaks the shape of the data.** Symlog axes above threshold; **all** standardisation uses median/IQR robust z, never mean/σ; outlier treatment disclosed. | Argentina missing from WB CPI @2025; Argentine and Venezuelan inflation in triple/quadruple digits dominates any σ-based standardisation. | `TEST-D-003` |
| **G-8** | **Nominal, real, and PPP answer different questions.** Unit strings including base year are mandatory on every axis and tooltip. `constant 2015 US$` and `constant 2021 int$` may never share an axis. Growth comes from the source's own local-currency-constant-price series, never recomputed from US$ levels. | WB real levels = constant **2015** US$ (embeds a 2015 FX snapshot in cross-country comparison); WB PPP volume = constant **2021** int$; ICP 2021 retrospectively revised historical PPP. | `TEST-M-003`, `TEST-M-004` |
| **G-9** | **Survivorship in the universe itself.** Historical aggregates and rank trajectories computed from the full panel with membership evaluated as of each year — never from today's Top 50. | "The top 50 grew X% since 1996" over today's members conditions on having become large. | `TEST-U-004` |
| **G-10** | **Denominator consistency.** Per-capita metrics come from the source's own published per-capita series. Where we compute a ratio, numerator and denominator share a source and vintage. | WB `SP.POP.TOTL` and WEO `LP` differ; a mixed ratio reconciles with neither source and users check. | `TEST-M-006` |
| **G-11** | **Currency-union non-independence.** `monetary_union` on the country dimension; correlation and regression modules expose clustering; the methodology page states it. | **10 of the 50** are euro-area members. Treating them as 10 independent draws on interest rates overstates precision. | — |
| **G-12** | **Correlation displays.** Show `n`; refuse a trendline below n=15; state the lag in lagged-view titles. "Growth Drivers" → **"Historical Associations"**; never *driver*, *cause*, or *effect* in UI copy. Claude reviews all such copy. | The generic "correlation ≠ causation" note is ignored on second sighting. Structural constraints work; disclaimers do not. | `D-013` |
| **G-13** | **Pin the comparison year.** Every ranking view exposes exactly one `comparison_year`; no view mixes observation years across rows. | Measured latest-year gaps: GDP missing UAE; CPI missing **US, Argentina**; exports missing **US, Japan**; investment missing **US, China, Japan**; internet use has only **6 of 50** at 2025. | `TEST-U-005` |
| **G-14** | **Stale latest year.** A metric whose latest usable year lags the reference year must display that year on the value, and must not appear in a view labelled with the reference year. | `NY.GDP.TOTL.RT.ZS` ends **2021**; R&D ends **2021**; central govt revenue ends **2022**; life expectancy ends **2024**. | `TEST-U-005` |
| **G-15** | **Never silently impute.** Interpolation, extrapolation and backfill are prohibited by default. Where analytically justified and approved by Claude, `raw_value` and `modeled_value` are both preserved and `observation_class = imputed`. | Master prompt §28. | `TEST-O-001` |
